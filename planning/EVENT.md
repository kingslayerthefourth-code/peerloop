## Requirements to preserve

The event accepts high school students aged 13–18 and individual entries. Substantial development belongs inside the event window; disclose earlier work and AI assistance. Entrants must understand their projects. Award consideration requires opting in and providing a publicly accessible project. The entrant handles consent and any prize paperwork.

## Pre-event boundary

Permitted preparation here: requirements, research, interface sketches, an implementation specification, verification scenarios, and draft submission materials. No application code, executable prototype, production design assets, automated tests, deployed site, or final demo is being prepared before the approved build start. Preserve these preparation files and disclose them when submitting.
